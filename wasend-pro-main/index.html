Privacy Policy

Extension Name: Wasend-Pro
Last Updated: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Wasend-Pro is a Chrome browser extension that helps users automate WhatsApp Web messaging, schedule broadcast messages, manage auto-replies, and integrate with WooCommerce stores. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about how data is handled.

2. Data We Collect

Wasend-Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers, except as described below:

  • License Key: When you activate the extension, your license key is sent to our licensing server (wasend-pro.vercel.app) only to verify its validity. No personal information is included in this request.
  • Contact Lists: Phone numbers and names you upload for broadcasts are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. They are never sent to our servers.
  • Message Templates: Templates you create are stored locally in IndexedDB. They are never uploaded to any server.
  • WooCommerce Credentials: If you connect a WooCommerce store, your store URL and API keys are stored locally in Chrome's storage API. They are used only to make direct API calls from your browser to your own store.
  • Activity Logs: Broadcast and auto-reply logs are stored locally in IndexedDB only. Logs older than 30 days are deleted automatically.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not read, store, or transmit your WhatsApp messages.
  • We do not access your WhatsApp account credentials or session data.
  • We do not collect browsing history or any data from websites other than web.whatsapp.com.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use analytics tools or tracking scripts of any kind.

4. Permissions Used and Why

  • storage / unlimitedStorage: To save your templates, contacts, settings, and logs locally on your device.
  • tabs: To detect the active WhatsApp Web tab and run automation only there.
  • scripting: To inject automation scripts into WhatsApp Web in order to send messages and manage chats.
  • alarms: To run scheduled tasks such as timed broadcasts and WooCommerce order polling.
  • notifications: To display desktop alerts for new WooCommerce orders.
  • host_permissions (web.whatsapp.com): Required to operate automation on WhatsApp Web.
  • host_permissions (https://*/*, http://*/*): Required to make API calls to user-configured WooCommerce stores, which may be hosted on any domain.

5. Third-Party Services

The extension communicates with the following external services:

  • wasend-pro.vercel.app: Our license verification server. Only your license key is transmitted to confirm it is valid.
  • Your WooCommerce store: Direct API calls are made from your browser to your own store URL using credentials you provide. We never see or store this data.

No other third-party services receive any data from this extension.

6. Data Storage and Retention

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage APIs (storage and IndexedDB). Activity logs are automatically deleted after 30 days. You can remove all stored data at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser storage.

7. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and not transmitted to our servers (except license key validation), the security of your data depends on your own device security. We do not have access to your data and cannot retrieve it.

8. Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
